This is a working prototype built on the free Open-Meteo APIs — current conditions, the 7-day forecast, air quality, the same-day-last-year comparison, and the multi-decade record are all live, real measurements (ERA5 reanalysis for the historical archive). Within-city areas use neighbourhood coordinates; at this resolution the differences between localities in one city are small and mostly honest framing for the UI.
